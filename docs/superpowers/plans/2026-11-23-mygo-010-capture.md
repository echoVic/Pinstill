## Benchmark

`go test ./internal/app -run '^$' -bench '^BenchmarkFinishAnnotationWithHistory$' -benchmem -benchtime=1s -count=5`

On Apple M2 Pro, with the same generated 3840 × 2160 PNG and history I/O enabled in both builds, the median fell from 246.8 to 160.0 ms/op and from 117.1 to 53.6 MiB allocated/op. The fixture replaces the clipboard call and excludes history reset from timing; it does not measure full desktop capture latency or peak RSS.
